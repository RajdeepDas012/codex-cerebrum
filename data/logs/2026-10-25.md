### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent the ABA problem during concurrent node deletion in a lock-free skip list?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to register nodes they are currently accessing so that reclaiming threads cannot free or reuse memory addresses until no thread holds a hazard pointer reference to them.  

**Q:** Why is multi-level pointer insertion in a skip list split into a bottom-level CAS followed by upper-level CAS updates?  
**A:** The bottom-level CAS officially inserts the node into the list, making it logically present. Upper-level pointers are then progressively swung into place, and failures at upper levels trigger a re-traversal rather than failing the entire insertion.
