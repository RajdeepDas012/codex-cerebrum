### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you prevent the ABA problem when performing atomic node-level-splicing in a lock-free skip list?  
**A:** By combining tagged pointers (or version counts) with hazard pointers to ensure that a memory address cannot be reused and silently swapped back while another thread is mid-operation.  

**Q:** Why is logical deletion required prior to physical deletion in concurrent lock-free skip lists?  
**A:** Logical deletion marks a node as removed by setting a flag on its pointer, preventing other threads from appending new child pointers to a node that is about to be spliced out.
