### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you handle concurrent metadata shifts during insertions in a lock-free quotient filter without using locks?  
**A:** By using fine-grained atomic Compare-And-Swap (CAS) primitives to update metadata bits (is_occupied, run_continuation, is_shifted) and retrying when a conflict is detected by another thread.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers preferred over standard reference counting in lock-free quotient filters?  
**A:** Hazard pointers prevent ABA problems and safely defer memory reclamation during high-frequency concurrent node insertions and cluster shifts without the performance overhead and contention of atomic reference counters.
