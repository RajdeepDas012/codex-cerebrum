### Summary
This topic covers the construction of a high-performance, lock-free bounded queue using atomic Compare-And-Swap (CAS) operations for queue indexing and hazard pointers for safe memory management. It enables high-throughput, contention-free producer-consumer coordination essential for multi-threaded task dispatching and asynchronous execution platforms.

- Guarantees linearizability and progress guarantees (lock-free or wait-free) under heavy multi-core concurrency.

### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you prevent the ABA problem when updating head and tail pointers in a lock-free queue?  
**A:** By using sequence numbers combined with pointers in a double-word CAS (DCAS) or tagging the pointer with an incrementing operation count.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers preferred over standard garbage collection in real-time concurrent queues?  
**A:** Hazard pointers provide deterministic, lock-free memory reclamation without the unpredictable pause times associated with tracing garbage collectors.
