### Interview Questions
**Q:** How does cuckoo hashing handle collision resolution, and why does it make concurrent lock-free implementation complex?  
**A:** Cuckoo hashing uses multiple hash functions to place keys in alternative buckets, evicting any key already occupying the target bucket. Concurrency is complex because evicting and migrating keys requires multi-step atomic updates across buckets to prevent race conditions during updates.  

**Q:** What role do hazard pointers play in a lock-free Cuckoo Hash Table during dynamic resizing?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to locally register pointers they are currently reading, ensuring that background resizing threads do not prematurely free or reclaim old bucket memory while concurrent lookups or migrations are still accessing them.
