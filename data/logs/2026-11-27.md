### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent race conditions during concurrent updates in a lock-free LSA index?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to locally register pointers they are currently accessing, blocking other threads from prematurely reclaiming or freeing those memory blocks until the read operation completes.
