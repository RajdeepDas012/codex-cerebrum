### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent the ABA problem and use-after-free issues in a lock-free treap?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to locally publish references to nodes they are currently accessing, blocking other threads from reclaiming those memory addresses until the reference is dropped.  

**Q:** Why are randomized priorities used in a treap instead of deterministic balancing mechanisms like AVL or Red-Black trees in a lock-free context?  
**A:** Randomized priorities decouple the balancing structure from the keys, simplifying local rotation logic and making it easier to implement using atomic CAS primitives without complex multi-node restructuring.
