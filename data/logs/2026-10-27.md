### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do atomic fingerprint-compacting and slot-migration CAS primitives prevent race conditions during concurrent updates in a Quotient-Summary Tree?  
**A:** They ensure that state changes to slots and fingerprints happen atomically, allowing threads to detect concurrent modifications and retry operations rather than blocking.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers necessary in a lock-free Quotient-Summary Tree instead of standard garbage collection or manual deletion?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to explicitly register nodes they are currently accessing, preventing other threads from prematurely freeing or recycling those memory blocks in a lock-free environment.
