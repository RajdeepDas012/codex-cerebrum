### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you maintain the leftist property (NPL of left child >= NPL of right child) safely using lock-free atomic operations?  
**A:** By computing the new NPL values locally during the merge phase and applying an atomic CAS to swap child pointers only if the structural state has not changed concurrently.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers necessary when merging nodes in a concurrent leftist heap?  
**A:** Hazard pointers protect nodes from being reclaimed by the garbage collection mechanism while other threads are simultaneously traversing or modifying their right spines.
