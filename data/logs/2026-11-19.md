### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you handle the global structural changes caused by splay rotations in a lock-free environment without blocking other threads?  
**A:** By using fine-grained atomic CAS operations on parent-child pointers combined with cooperative helping or retry mechanisms, alongside hazard pointers to safely manage node lifecycles.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers necessary when implementing a lock-free Splay Tree?  
**A:** Hazard pointers prevent a thread from freeing or reusing a node's memory while another concurrent thread is still traversing or modifying pointers referencing that node.
