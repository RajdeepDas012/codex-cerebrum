### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do metadata bits (is_occupied, is_continuation, is_shifted) facilitate lock-free insertions in a Quotient Filter?  
**A:** They delineate runs and quotient clusters without explicit locks, allowing threads to use atomic CAS operations to shift metadata and slots when resolving collisions.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers required in a concurrent lock-free Quotient Filter implementation?  
**A:** Hazard pointers safely manage concurrent memory reclamation during dynamic resizing and slot reorganization, preventing threads from reading freed memory blocks while other threads modify cluster layouts.
