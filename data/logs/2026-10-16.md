### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you handle the ABA problem when multiple threads concurrently modify parent and child nodes in a lock-free priority queue heap?  
**A:** By using tagged pointers (combining a version counter with the memory address) or utilizing hazard pointers to ensure nodes are not prematurely freed while another thread's CAS operation is pending.  

**Q:** Why are traditional mutex-based priority queues inefficient for high-core-count task dispatchers?

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers preferred over garbage collection or simple reference counting in lock-free concurrent maps?
