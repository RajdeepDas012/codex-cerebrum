### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent race conditions when freeing nodes in a lock-free trie?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to publish references to nodes they are currently accessing, ensuring that memory reclaimers cannot free those nodes until no hazard pointers reference them.  

**Q:** What is the primary performance bottleneck of using standard mutex locks in a high-speed routing table, and how does a lock-free trie solve it?  
**A:** Mutex locks cause thread blocking and context-switching overhead under heavy multi-core contention. Lock-free tries eliminate blocking by using atomic CAS primitives for optimistic updates.
