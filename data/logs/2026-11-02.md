### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent the ABA problem during concurrent node removal in a lock-free heap?  
**A:** Hazard pointers explicitly mark nodes as actively accessed by a thread, preventing the memory allocator from recycling or freeing those nodes until all referencing threads release their hazards.  

**Q:** Why are Fibonacci heaps challenging to implement in a lock-free concurrent context?  
**A:** Their complex structural adjustments, such as cascading cuts and global consolidation steps during extraction, involve widespread pointer modifications that are difficult to coordinate atomically across multiple threads without severe contention.
