### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent memory reclamation races during concurrent updates in a lock-free Wavelet Tree?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to publish pointers they are currently reading to a thread-local list, ensuring that memory allocators do not reclaim nodes until no thread holds an active hazard pointer referencing them.  

**Q:** Why are standard mutexes inefficient when executing parallel rank-select queries on a Wavelet Tree?  
**A:** Mutexes introduce thread serialization and context-switch overheads at critical bottlenecks (especially near the root node), whereas lock-free CAS primitives allow multiple reader threads to traverse and query concurrently without blocking.
