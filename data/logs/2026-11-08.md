### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers ensure safe memory reclamation in a lock-free suffix array?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to publish references they are currently accessing so that other threads know not to free those memory blocks until the references are cleared.  

**Q:** Why are standard comparison-based sorting algorithms insufficient for lock-free parallel suffix array construction?  
**A:** They often require global state modification or coarse locks, which introduce bottlenecks and violate lock-free progress guarantees.
