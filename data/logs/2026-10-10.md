### Interview Questions
**Q:** How does a Count-Min Sketch trade accuracy for space efficiency?  
**A:** It uses a hash-based 2D matrix where multiple hash functions map large keys to smaller counter indices, resulting in space-efficient frequency estimations that may occasionally overestimate due to collisions.  

**Q:** Why are lock-free atomic operations preferred over mutex locks in high-throughput streaming systems?  
**A:** Mutex locks can cause thread contention, context switching overhead, and priority inversion, whereas lock-free atomic primitives allow threads to make progress independently without blocking.
