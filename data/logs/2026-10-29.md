bool update_mbr(RTreeNode* node, const BoundingBox& new_box) {
    BoundingBox old_box;
    do {
        old_box = node->mbr.load(std::memory_order_acquire);
        BoundingBox combined = old_box.union_with(new_box);

}

**Q:** What is the primary role of hazard pointers in a concurrent spatial index like an R-Tree?
