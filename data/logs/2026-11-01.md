**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent memory reclamation issues in a lock-free R-Tree?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to atomically register nodes they are currently accessing so that other threads know not to deallocate or free those nodes until the reader unregisters its hazard pointer.  

**Q:** Why are standard lock-based approaches inefficient for high-throughput spatial indexing?  
**A:** Global or even fine-grained locks cause thread contention, serialization bottlenecks, and potential deadlocks when multiple cores simultaneously modify overlapping bounding boxes in a hierarchical tree.
