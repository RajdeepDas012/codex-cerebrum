### Best Practices
- ✅ Always wrap traversal pointers with thread-local hazard pointer guards before reading node successor fields.
- ✅ Mark deleted nodes logically with a marked bit on the forward pointer before physically unlinking them via CAS.
- ✅ Keep tower height generation cryptographically or pseudorandomly bounded to prevent excessive memory overhead and cache misses.

### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you prevent a thread from freeing a node while another thread is actively traversing through its forward pointers?  
**A:** By employing a hazard pointer mechanism where the reading thread registers the node address in a thread-local hazard pointer slot before dereferencing, ensuring memory reclaimers defer deletion until the pointer is cleared.  

**Q:** Why are logical deletions required in lock-free Skip Lists before physical unlinking?  
**A:** Logical deletion via a marked pointer bit prevents concurrent inserters from updating pointers on a node that is currently being removed from the list, avoiding race conditions and structural corruption.
