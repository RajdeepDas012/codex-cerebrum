### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do atomic peer-routing and membership vectors prevent deadlocks in a lock-free Skip Graph?  
**A:** By avoiding coarse-grained locks entirely and utilizing fine-grained atomic compare-and-swap (CAS) primitives to update individual pointers and membership states, threads never wait indefinitely for blocked resources.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers essential when implementing lock-free data structures like a Skip Graph?  
**A:** Hazard pointers protect dynamically deleted nodes from being immediately reclaimed by the memory allocator while other concurrent threads are actively traversing pointers referencing those nodes.
