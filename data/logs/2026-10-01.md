### Best Practices
- ✅ Use relaxed memory ordering for non-dependent reads, but enforce release-acquire semantics around CAS state transitions.

- ✅ Integrate a robust epoch-based or hazard-pointer garbage collection mechanism to safely reclaim orphaned spatial nodes.

### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you prevent use-after-free issues when multiple threads traverse and modify a concurrent BVH simultaneously?  
**A:** By implementing a memory reclamation scheme like hazard pointers, which allows threads to register nodes they are currently accessing so collectors know when it is safe to deallocate them.  

**Q:** Why are standard mutexes inefficient for maintaining a dynamically updating Bounding Volume Hierarchy in high-fps rendering engines?  
**A:** Mutexes introduce global serialization and thread blocking, severely limiting scalability and throughput when hundreds of threads concurrently update spatial bounds and cast rays.
