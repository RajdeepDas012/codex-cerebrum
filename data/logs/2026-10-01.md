### Best Practices
- ✅ Always register active nodes under hazard pointers before traversing child pointers in a lock-free structure.
- ✅ Design key-redirection logic to be idempotent so failed CAS attempts can be safely retried without corrupting state.

### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent memory reclamation issues in a lock-free Bx-Tree?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to declare which nodes they are currently accessing, ensuring that no other thread frees or reclaims memory for those nodes until they are unlinked and safe to delete.  

**Q:** Why are lock-free atomic key-redirections preferred over mutual exclusion locks in append-only storage systems?  
**A:** They eliminate lock contention, thread blockages, and priority inversion, allowing multiple cores to perform read and write operations concurrently with bounded latency.
