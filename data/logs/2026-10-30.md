### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you handle race conditions during a multi-step splay rotation using only CAS primitives?  
**A:** By validating that pointer links remain unchanged throughout the rotation sequence, and retrying the operation using optimistic concurrency control if a conflict is detected.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers necessary when implementing a lock-free Splay Tree?  
**A:** Hazard pointers prevent a thread from freeing memory of a node that another thread is currently inspecting or repositioning during a concurrent splay rotation.
