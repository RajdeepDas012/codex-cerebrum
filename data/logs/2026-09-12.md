### Key Points

def simulate_cascade(prices, leverage, threshold):

### Code Example

import pandas as pd
def detect_vsa_anomaly(df):

### Summary
Stopping volume occurs when a sharp downward price move is met with exceptionally high volume and a wide spread, yet the price fails to close near its lows. ### Key Points

- Accompanied by unusually high or ultra-high volume, showing massive participation.
- Represents institutional absorption where institutional buyers step in to soak up panic selling from retail investors.
- Serves as an early warning sign that a downtrend is losing momentum and a bottoming process may be beginning.

### Code Example
*Identifies potential stopping volume bars based on volume spikes, wide spreads, and strong closes relative to the range.*

```python
import pandas as pd

def detect_stopping_volume(df):
    df['spread'] = df['High'] - df['Low']
    df['avg_volume'] = df['Volume'].rolling(20).mean()
    df['close_location'] = (df['Close'] - df['Low']) / (df['Spread'] + 1e-9)
    
    # Condition: High volume, wide spread, down trend bar closing off the lows
    stopping_vol = (
        (df['Volume'] > 2.0 * df['avg_volume']) &
        (df['spread'] > df['spread'].rolling(20).mean()) &
        (df['Close'] < df['Open']) &
        (df['close_location'] > 0.4)
    )
    return df[stopping_vol]
```
