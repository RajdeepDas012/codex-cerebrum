### Summary

### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do atomic summary pointers and cluster indexing eliminate locks in a concurrent vEB tree?  
**A:** They allow threads to independently update subtree status bits and summary indicators via compare-and-swap (CAS) operations rather than acquiring global or regional mutexes.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers necessary when implementing a lock-free vEB tree?  
**A:** Hazard pointers protect nodes from being reclaimed by the memory allocator while another thread is actively traversing references to them, preventing use-after-free race conditions.
