### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you handle safe memory reclamation when multiple threads concurrently restructure nodes in a lock-free Wavelet Tree?  
**A:** By employing hazard pointers where threads register references to nodes they are currently traversing, preventing reclamation until no thread holds a hazard pointer to the target node.  

**Q:** Why are atomic rank and select primitives critical for concurrent Wavelet Trees?  
**A:** They allow threads to navigate through the tree levels without acquiring global locks, maintaining high throughput for parallel text searches and frequency queries.
