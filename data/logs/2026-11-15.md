```cpp
    curr->right = rightChild->left;
    rightChild->left = curr;
    return rightChild;
}
```

### Best Practices
- ✅ Always acquire hazard pointers before traversing nodes that might be unlinked and freed by competing threads.
- ✅ Keep atomic retry loops concise to minimize contention overhead during heavy concurrent write workloads.

### Interview Questions
