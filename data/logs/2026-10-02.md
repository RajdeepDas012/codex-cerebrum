### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do atomic CAS primitives prevent race conditions during peer-pointer routing in a concurrent Skip Graph?  
**A:** CAS primitives ensure that pointer updates only succeed if the expected node state has not been modified by another thread since it was last read, avoiding lost updates.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers necessary when deleting nodes from a lock-free Skip Graph?  
**A:** Hazard pointers protect concurrently accessed nodes from being reclaimed by the memory allocator while other threads are still traversing their pointers.
