### Interview Questions
**Q:** Why use Fetch-And-Add (FAA) instead of pure CAS loops for queue enqueue operations?  
**A:** FAA allows threads to atomically reserve a unique slot index without contention loops, dramatically reducing cache thrashing compared to repeatedly failing CAS operations on a single tail pointer.  

**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent the ABA problem in lock-free priority queues?  
**A:** Hazard pointers explicitly publish which node a thread is currently accessing, preventing other threads from reclaiming or recycling that memory address until the referencing thread clears its hazard pointer.
