### Interview Questions
**Q:** How does a Cuckoo Filter differ from a Bloom Filter, and why does lock-free concurrency implementation present unique challenges?  
**A:** Cuckoo Filters support deletions and store fingerprints rather than multiple bit positions, but concurrent relocations (kick-outs) require complex CAS loops to avoid race conditions.  

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers essential when implementing lock-free Cuckoo Filters?  
**A:** Hazard pointers protect dynamically relocated or resized buckets from being freed by another thread while a worker is actively evaluating a kick-out path.
