### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do you prevent a thread from freeing a skip list node while another thread is traversing through it?  
**A:** By using hazard pointers to register the node as actively accessed by the current thread before reading its pointers, ensuring memory reclamation is deferred until no threads hold a hazard pointer to it.  

**Q:** What is the purpose of logically marking a forward pointer before physically splicing out a node in a lock-free skip list?  
**A:** Logical marking informs other concurrent threads that the node is deleted, preventing them from adding new pointers to it while helper threads or the deleter complete the physical removal across all levels.
