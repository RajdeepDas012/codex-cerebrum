### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do right-links in a B-Link Tree prevent the need for global locks during concurrent node splits?

**Q:** Why are hazard pointers necessary when implementing a lock-free B-Link Tree?  
**A:** Hazard pointers protect dynamically freed nodes from being reclaimed by the memory allocator while other concurrent reader or writer threads are still traversing or modifying them.
