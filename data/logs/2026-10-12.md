### Best Practices
- ✅ Always acquire hazard pointer protection on target nodes prior to dereferencing their forward pointers in multi-threaded contexts.
- ✅ Implement careful epoch-based or hazard-pointer-based deferred reclamation lists to safely clean up unlinked tower levels.

### Interview Questions
**Q:** How do hazard pointers prevent race conditions during lock-free skip list deletions?  
**A:** Hazard pointers allow threads to register nodes they are currently accessing so that deleting threads know to defer memory reclamation until no other thread holds a reference to those nodes.  

**Q:** Why are multi-level forward pointers advantageous in a concurrent skip list compared to a standard linked list?  
**A:** They reduce traversal length from O(n) to O(log n) by allowing threads to skip over large segments of elements, reducing contention and improving multi-core scalability.
